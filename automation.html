SERVICE 03 · AI & AUTOMATION

Stop Answering The Same Message Twice

Every business gets the same handful of questions on repeat — prices, hours, availability, how to order. Anchor builds simple automation that answers instantly, captures orders properly, and connects the tools you already use, so your time goes into running the business, not repeating yourself.

What's included

  • Auto-replies for common customer questions on WhatsApp or your website
  • Structured order capture, so orders land in one place instead of scattered across chats
  • One workflow connected to a tool you already use — for example, orders logged automatically to a shared sheet
  • Setup and testing using your real, actual questions and scenarios
  • A short walkthrough so you can update responses yourself afterward

Who this is for

Businesses fielding a high volume of repetitive WhatsApp, DM, or phone questions — fashion sellers, restaurants, service businesses — losing time, and sometimes losing customers who message and never get a fast enough reply.

How we build it

  1. Identify the actual repetitive questions, and where they come in
  2. Map the one workflow worth automating first — we start with one, not everything at once
  3. Build and test it against real examples from your business
  4. Refine the wording based on how customers actually respond
  5. Launch and monitor the early results together

Common questions

Will this replace me talking to customers?

No. It handles the repetitive first-contact stuff — prices, hours, "do you have X" — so you spend your time on the conversations that actually need a person.

What tools can it connect to?

Most commonly WhatsApp, email, and simple spreadsheets. If you use something specific, mention it and we'll confirm it's workable before quoting.

Can it grow into more automation later?

Yes — this package covers one solid workflow. Once you're handling several connected processes, that usually shifts into a Custom Software project.

Can I see an example first?

Yes — our Auto-FAQ demo shows the kind of instant-response tool we build.
