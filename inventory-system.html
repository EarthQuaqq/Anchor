SERVICE 02 · INVENTORY & SALES SYSTEM

Know What's In Stock. Every Time You Check.

Stock says one thing, the shelf says another. Anchor builds simple stock and sales tracking systems so you always know what's moving, what's running low, and what needs reordering — without an accountant or a spreadsheet expert on staff.

What's included

  • A product catalog with quantities, pricing, and categories
  • Sales recording that automatically updates stock levels
  • Low-stock alerts, so you reorder before you run out — not after
  • Basic sales reports: daily, weekly, and monthly totals, plus best sellers
  • Single-branch setup (multi-branch handled under Custom Software)
  • Staff logins, so more than one person can use it properly
  • A training session so your team can start using it immediately

Who this is for

Shops and small retailers — electronics, fashion, hardware, general dealers — currently tracking stock on paper or in someone's head, and losing money to overselling, missed reorders, or shortages nobody can explain.

How we build it

  1. Walk through your actual stock process — how items come in, how they're sold, who's involved
  2. Set up a product catalog structured the way you actually think about your stock
  3. Build and test with real sample data from your business
  4. Train your team on day-to-day use
  5. Go live, with support through the first weeks of real use

Common questions

What if I have more than one location?

Multi-branch stock needs a different structure and is handled as Custom Software — mention it when you get in touch and we'll scope it properly.

Does it need internet to work?

It runs in the browser, so a basic connection is needed. If your connectivity is unreliable, tell us — we can talk through what's realistic for your setup.

Will it stop me from overselling items I don't have?

Yes. Stock decreases automatically as sales are recorded, and low-stock alerts flag items before they run out completely.

Can I see this in action first?

Yes — our Inventory Management System demo is built in the same style as what you'd get.
