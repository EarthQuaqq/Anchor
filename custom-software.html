SERVICE 04 · CUSTOM SOFTWARE

When Off-The-Shelf Doesn't Fit Your Business

Some businesses outgrow templates and simple tools fast — multiple staff roles, several locations, workflows nobody else has. Anchor builds custom systems shaped around how your business actually runs, not the other way around.

What's included

  • Multi-user access with different permission levels — owner, manager, staff
  • Custom reports built around what you actually need to track
  • Integrations with tools you already use
  • Built around your specific workflow, not squeezed into a generic template
  • A scoping session before any pricing is finalized, so there are no surprises

Who this is for

Businesses with workflows that have outgrown spreadsheets, WhatsApp, or simple tools — multi-branch operations, businesses needing several staff roles, or a process specific enough that nothing "off-the-shelf" quite fits.

How we build it

  1. A deep-dive scoping session to fully understand the workflow — this is what determines the real price, not a guess upfront
  2. A written scope and fixed quote before any work starts
  3. Built in stages with check-ins, not a single reveal at the end
  4. Tested with real data from your business
  5. Launch, team training, and support through the first weeks of real use

Common questions

Why isn't there a fixed price like the other services?

Custom systems vary hugely in scope. A quote after a scoping conversation is the only way to give you an honest number instead of one that changes later.

How long does a custom project take?

It depends on complexity — timelines are discussed and agreed upfront, with milestone check-ins so you're never left waiting in the dark.

Can I see an example of custom work?

Yes — our Quote & Invoice Generator is one example of software purpose-built around a specific business need.
