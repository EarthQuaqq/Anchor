SERVICE 01 · BUSINESS WEBSITE

A Website That Actually Brings You Customers

Most small business websites are digital business cards nobody visits twice. Anchor builds ones people actually use — to check what you sell, see your prices, message you on WhatsApp, and decide to trust you before they've even walked through your door.

What's included

  • 5 custom-designed pages (Home, About, Services or Products, Gallery, Contact)
  • Mobile-first design — most of your visitors will find you on a phone, not a laptop
  • Fast loading pages, without bloated templates slowing things down
  • Contact form and a one-tap WhatsApp button built in
  • Basic on-page SEO, so Google can actually find and understand your business
  • Hosting setup included, plus a walkthrough on managing the site afterward
  • One round of revisions before launch

Who this is for

Growing businesses that currently rely only on WhatsApp or a Facebook page — and are quietly losing customers who search for them on Google, find nothing, and move on to a competitor who showed up instead.

How we build it

  1. A short call or questionnaire about your business and customers
  2. Planning what pages you need and what goes where
  3. A design draft for your feedback before anything is finalized
  4. Building the site and connecting your forms and WhatsApp
  5. One review round, then launch and handover

Most website projects take about 1–2 weeks, depending on how quickly content and feedback come back.

Common questions

Do I need to provide my own content and photos?

We work with what you have. If you don't have professional photos yet, we'll advise on what's usable — and can help write the copy if needed.

What if I need more than 5 pages?

Extra pages can be added and quoted upfront, so there are no surprises once work is underway.

Can I update the site myself afterward?

We walk you through basic updates at handover. If you'd rather not manage it yourself, Anchor can also handle ongoing updates for a small fee.

Do you handle the domain and hosting?

Yes — hosting setup is included. We can register your domain too, or you can keep it under your own name for full ownership.
