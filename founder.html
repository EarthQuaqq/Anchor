Founder, Anchor

I'm Alpha. I've watched businesses lose money to stock that doesn't add up, orders lost between WhatsApp and notebooks, and hours burned every week on admin a simple system could handle in seconds.

Most of that isn't a people problem — it's a tools problem. The business grows faster than the systems behind it, and nobody has time to stop and fix that while still running things day to day.

I built Anchor to close that gap — real, working tools for real businesses, without the slow timelines or bloated pricing bigger agencies charge. Every project gets my full attention, from understanding what's actually costing you time to building something you'll actually use.
